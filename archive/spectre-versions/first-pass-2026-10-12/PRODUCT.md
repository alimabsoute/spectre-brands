# Spectre Brands

## Register

brand

## Users

Readers exploring sourced histories of dead companies and brands that survive as a name. Each post-mortem offers an overview, then the evidence and the longer story.

## Product purpose

Explain what a brand sold, how it failed, and what survives. Facts, citations and uncertainty remain accessible.

## Brand personality

Editorial, archival, specific. The house voice follows docs/writing-rules/VOICE-SPECTRE.md.

## Anti-references

The current task rejects tall, text-heavy openers and dead space. Preserve the accepted lower-page format and animated graphs.

## Design principles

Lead with the brand and a concise summary. Put a meaningful animated visual in the first screen. Keep full context behind clear, accessible disclosures. Preserve sources, qualifiers and substantive content.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Respect reduced motion, keyboard access, readable contrast and 320px viewports. Content must work without JavaScript. Reserve visual dimensions to avoid layout shift.
