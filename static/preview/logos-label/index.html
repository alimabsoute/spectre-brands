Centre label × peeking ghost: 5 ranked variants + 8 label ideas

Lead base: X23’s ghost peeking from under a peeled corner of the orange strip, on X06’s black shell with X06’s marker lettering. A researched 78 × 46 mm centre label sits in the moulded recess between the reel windows (recess 80.2 × 48 mm). Each design comes worn (yellowed and sun-faded paper, peeling corners, sticker residue, scuffs, marker smudges) and clean. All facts on the labels were checked against the site on Oct 8, 2026: 38 post-mortems, 15 dead, 23 ghost, 7 aisles, 1,174 cited sources. Label research and sources · Contact sheet (JPG) · Close-up: best label · Close-up: best variant. Design preview only, not in production.

Most cohesive: the store's own rental copy. Marker title and orange footer echo the orange strip, DEAD/GHOST boxes replace SP/LP/EP, plus a barcode. It reads instantly as an 80s rental tape and describes the site in one line.
