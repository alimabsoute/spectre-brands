Final tape: 10 ghost postures × hand-written index labels, ranked

Base: X06 black shell and "Spectre Brands" marker lettering on a worn orange strip. The ghost peeks up from under the strip’s peeled top-left corner. The centre label is a hand-written index listing only the seven site categories (Dot-com & internet, Electronics retail, Toy stores, Food & drink, Video games, Film & home video, Restaurants), with no counts, under a different header on each variant. Every design comes worn and clean, with icons at 64/32/16 px.
