Realistic VHS cassette: 25 concepts

Round 3: a realistic VHS tape drawn to real proportions (187 × 103 × 25 mm; front face ≈ 1.82:1, spine ≈ 7.5:1), with the Spectre Brands logo on the label, a marker label, a rental sticker, the spine, or above the cassette. Ghosts are optional and subtle. Each lockup is shown on cream and on black, with its mark at 64, 32 and 16 px. Flat inks and outlined lettering; design preview only, not in production.
